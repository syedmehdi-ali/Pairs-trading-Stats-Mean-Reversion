Methodology

STEP 01

Cointegration Test

Engle-Granger two-step test verifies the pair has a stable long-run equilibrium relationship.

STEP 02

Spread Construction

OLS regression finds the hedge ratio β. Spread = KO − β·PEP − α, which should be mean-reverting.

STEP 03

Signal Generation

Rolling z-score standardises the spread. Entry at ±2σ, exit at ±0.5σ. Dollar-neutral positions.

STEP 04

Out-of-Sample Test

Parameters fit on 60% training data. Strategy evaluated on unseen 40% to guard against overfitting.
